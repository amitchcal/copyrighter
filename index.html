Proprietary Knowledge Base (7 Frameworks)

The core cognitive architectures powering the viral copywriter intelligence engine.

⚡ 1. The 2-Second Hook Architecture

The visual, auditory, and cognitive alignment required to stop the thumb on short-form feeds within 1.8 to 2.2 seconds.

[Visual Pattern Interrupt] + [Auditory Counter-Statement] + [Text on Screen Curiosity Gap]

⚡ 2. PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solution)

The classic tension-and-relief narrative spine refined for fast-paced modern algorithmic feeds.

[Unspoken Struggle] -> [Cost of Inaction / Future Pain] -> [Frictionless Tactical Fix]

⚡ 3. The Anti-Cliché Human Diagnostic

Eliminates artificial corporate fluff and replaces generic statements with raw, visceral, highly specific proof points.

Replace "Increase efficiency" with "Cut 3 hours from your Sunday evening"

⚡ 4. The Open-Loop Curiosity Framework

Plants an unresolved cognitive puzzle in the viewer's subconscious that forces completion until the final reveal.

[Unexpected Outcome] + [Hidden Mechanism] + [Delay Reward until Step 3]

⚡ 5. High-Status Authority Framing

Establishes immediate non-needy credibility by diagnosing errors rather than begging for attention.

[Peer Diagnosis] + [Uncompromising Standard] + [Calm Delivery]

⚡ 6. The 3-Beat Retention Spine

Prevents mid-video dropoff by delivering continuous micro-payoffs every 7 to 10 seconds.

Beat 1 (The Context) -> Beat 2 (The Twist / Nuance) -> Beat 3 (The Actionable Rule)

⚡ 7. The Irresistible Micro-CTA

Minimizes psychological friction by asking for a simple 1-word keyword comment that triggers automated DM delivery.

"Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you the exact [ASSET] for free."
